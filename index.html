Day by day

Morning is first light to 10 AM, midday 10 AM to 3 PM, afternoon 3 PM to sunset. Tap a row for the full forecast.

Forecast from Open-Meteo (NOAA HRRR and GFS models). Alerts and Keene observations from the National Weather Service, Albany. River flow from USGS gauge 01154500 at North Walpole, water temperature from 01161280 near Northfield. River forecast from the NWS Northeast River Forecast Center. Air quality from Open-Meteo (Copernicus CAMS), and the model comparison from GFS, ECMWF, GEM and ICON via Open-Meteo.

Thresholds are starting points, not club rules. Lightning: off the water at the first thunder, back on 30 minutes after the last. Look at the water before you launch.
